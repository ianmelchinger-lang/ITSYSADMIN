Weigh the Claim Against the Probe and the Place

A test reports on the probe it sends, from the place where it runs. The probe is what the test actually sends: an echo request, a question put to one name source, or a real request for the page. The place is the machine it runs from. A claim in a ticket can be written only as strongly as those two allow.

The storefront of a mid-market retailer sits on the host cld-lnx-01. Staff at a regional office report that they cannot open its order screen. Each claim below comes with one result.
