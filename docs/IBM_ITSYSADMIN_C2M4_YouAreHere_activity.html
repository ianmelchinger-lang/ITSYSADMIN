A match count tells you how many lines mentioned a word. It doesn't tell you how many distinct things were behind those lines — a till retrying every two seconds produces hundreds of matching lines and zero customers, and nothing in the file converts one to the other on its own.

Getting from 6,000 to the real number takes a different move: naming the field that identifies the thing you actually want to count, and counting the distinct values in it. That's not about which search term you use. It's a capability — treating a file's output as something you reduce on a stated rule, rather than something you read off the screen — and it's what the rest of this module builds.
