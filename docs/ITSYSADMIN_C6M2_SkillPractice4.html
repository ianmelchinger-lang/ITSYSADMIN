Check the Record Against the Method

A runbook entry is the record a diagnosis leaves for the next person. It ties the reported symptom to the located link and to the action taken. An entry can read well and still show a method that broke down, and the next person inherits whatever it gets wrong.

The five steps a sound entry records

  1. Define. Restate the report as one exact request: who, from where, to what name and port, since when.
  2. Isolate. Choose tests that split the chain. Change nothing until the link is located.
  3. Test. Make one change at a time, and predict the result before looking.
  4. Resolve. Repeat the user's own request, from where the user made it.
  5. Document. Record the symptom, the located link, the action, and what remains. A root cause is the condition that has to go for the failure to stop recurring. A fix that restores service while that condition stays is a workaround.
