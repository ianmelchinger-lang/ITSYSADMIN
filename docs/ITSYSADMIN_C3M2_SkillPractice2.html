How access reaches the user

Access to the warehouse folder was granted once, to a group. A group is an object in the directory with members, and a right granted to it reaches every member by a route: their membership. The group also carries a scope, a setting on the group that decides where its access can be applied.

Granted once: members of grp-warehouse-ops may read the warehouse folder.

Nothing you do here edits this grant.

Two legs decide whether that grant reaches a given account.

  • Membership route. Is the account in the group the grant names?
  • Scope. Does the folder sit where that group's access can be applied?
