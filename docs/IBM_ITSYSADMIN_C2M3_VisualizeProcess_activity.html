Order the Answer

Six lines describe the whole path through this module's problem: where it starts, the moves that answer it, and the signal that says it's actually answered. They're out of order.

Use the arrows to put them in an order that could actually work. More than one correct order exists — you're not hunting for one exact sequence, just one that doesn't put anything before something it depends on.

    This works because you can't confirm a target you haven't stated, and you can't close the case on evidence you haven't read. Whether you read "running" or "enabled" first never mattered — they're separate facts, from separate commands, and neither depends on the other.

    That's the whole shape of the module: name what you're trying to confirm, read each fact on its own evidence, then show both hold before you call it done.
