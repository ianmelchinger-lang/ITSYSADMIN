Read a week of the log

A completion record is a dated line that a job appends to its log when its work is finished. Here is a week of one. The job, capacity-report, runs at 02:00 on hq-win-01 as svc-backup, and nobody is signed in. Each night it should measure 3 shares and append one line that says so. Beside each night’s log you can see what the scheduler recorded, in plain words.

For each night, decide whether the log shows that the work was done, or whether the night is a finding to chase. The free-space figures are illustrative output of the job’s own script. They claim nothing about a real system, and none of them decides a marking. Nothing here is scored.

Who reads this log, and how often?

A named person reads the log on a stated cadence, compares the dated lines with what was expected, and treats a missing or mismatched line as a finding. The cadence decides how long a finding sits before anyone sees it. How often to read is the organization’s choice, so there is no single right answer here. Choose a cadence and read what it costs.
