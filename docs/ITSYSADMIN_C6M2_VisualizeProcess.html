Assemble the Path from Report to Record

A report that says only "it will not connect" has to travel to one located link, a confirmed repair, and a record the next person can read. The order of the moves matters more than any single move. A sound move made at the wrong point can still mislead.

Build the whole path, from the problem to the signal that the work succeeded. Use the arrow buttons to move a card. Two cards do not belong on the path: mark them to leave them out. More than one order can be sound, and the check flags only orders that break a dependency.
