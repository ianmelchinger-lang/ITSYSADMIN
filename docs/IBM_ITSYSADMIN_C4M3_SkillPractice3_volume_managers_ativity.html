Skill Practice — Volume Managers, Same Move

Different vendor, same three questions.

Adding capacity at the block layer is the move you already have. A volume manager just does that move with a layer of indirection in between — raw disks gather into a group, and a piece of that group is what a filesystem actually sits on. For each term below, decide which part of that chain it names.

Linux — LVM

Physical volume
Volume group
Logical volume

Windows — Storage Spaces
